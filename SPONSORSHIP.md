# Supporting Unknown Engine

Unknown Engine is maintained by [pr0dus](https://github.com/pr0dus). This public repository makes the scientific implementation, disclosed evidence, reproduction instructions and next research questions available for assessment.

[GitHub Sponsors profile](https://github.com/sponsors/pr0dus). As of this publication, the profile has been prepared, but its current approval state has not been reconfirmed. A visible funding link does not establish that sponsorship payments are available.

## What support funds

- Maintaining the runnable source, verifiers and documentation.
- Improving the worked examples and incorporating reproducible bug reports.
- Developing new cases and stronger cheap baselines before spending on model trials.
- Preparing an independently operated comparison that can reveal a useful agent failure.

Paid model trials would require a specific protocol, an explicit budget and a spending limit. No new inference budget or delivery date is promised by this release.

The current results include method/raw-data equivalence, unfavorable single-recipient acquisition costs and simple-script saturation. Funding will not change how those findings are reported.

## Existing tier design

The configured tier amounts are USD: $5 monthly, $15 monthly and $10 one-time. All support the same public work. These descriptions record the prepared profile; the live GitHub page controls availability and payment terms.

There is no custom consulting, exclusive research result, automatic private-repository access, guaranteed feature, discovery or timetable. Sponsors do not influence scores, comparisons or conclusions. Participation and criticism do not require sponsorship. Updates will describe meaningful completed milestones, including failures and negative results; no fixed newsletter schedule is promised.
